A better starting point

Understand. Prepare. Talk.

Good support is not about having the perfect words. It is about having a safe place to make sense of what’s happening.

1

Understand

Use the guides to put everyday experiences into clearer language.

2

Prepare

Save questions and observations you want to bring to a professional.

3

Talk

Book a consultation and decide together what kind of care may fit.

What therapy can be

A conversation, not a performance.

You can arrive unsure, overwhelmed, or without the right words. A first conversation can simply be a place to ask questions.

Before you book

Questions worth asking a therapist

  • What approaches do you use for the concerns I’m bringing?
  • How do you decide what to work on first?
  • How will we know whether therapy is helping?
  • What are your fees, availability, privacy practices and telehealth rules?

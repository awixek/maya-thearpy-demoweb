About the practice

A warm, direct approach to therapy.

The practice describes care focused on anxiety, trauma, burnout and perfectionism, with an emphasis on collaborative conversations and practical progress.

Clinical approach

Care should make sense to the person receiving it.

The original practice information lists CBT, EMDR, mindfulness and body-based approaches. The appropriate approach depends on the person’s concerns, history, goals and clinical assessment.

Collaborative

Questions and preferences are part of the conversation.

Practical

Work can connect insight with concrete skills and next steps.

Individualized

Different concerns may call for different approaches.

Progress-aware

Check-ins can help clarify what is changing and what still needs attention.

First conversation

You can come with questions.

The practice describes a free 15-minute consultation to discuss what is bringing you in, ask questions and consider fit.

FAQ

Questions, answered simply.

Practical information before you decide whether to reach out.

What happens in the first consultation?

The practice describes a relaxed 15-minute conversation to discuss what is bringing you to therapy, ask questions and consider whether the fit feels right.

Is online therapy available?

The practice information says telehealth is available across California. Confirm current licensure, location requirements and availability before booking.

Which therapy approach is right for me?

There is no single answer. A qualified clinician can consider your concerns, goals, history and preferences and discuss appropriate options.

Can the AI Support Guide diagnose me?

No. It is for general education and navigation only. Do not use it for diagnosis, emergencies or urgent clinical decisions.

What should I ask before starting therapy?

Ask about the clinician’s approach, experience with your concerns, fees, availability, privacy practices, telehealth rules and how progress will be reviewed.

What if I’m not sure therapy is right for me?

A consultation can be used to ask questions without committing to ongoing therapy. You can discuss what you hope would change and what kind of support you are looking for.
